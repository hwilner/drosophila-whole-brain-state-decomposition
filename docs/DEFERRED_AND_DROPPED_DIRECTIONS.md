# Deferred and Dropped Directions

This document records public-release boundaries rather than a research outcome.

## Deferred work

The following work is outside the present public tree: data acquisition and handling; assessment of candidate materials; data-dependent preprocessing; empirical state-decomposition experiments; visualizations based on research artifacts; and interpretation or reporting of outcomes. Deferral does not imply that any direction succeeded, failed, or was attempted.

## Dropped from this release

Result-bearing narratives, figure specifications and assets, internal archive indexes, third-party metadata, access logs, raw or derived artifacts, and tests coupled to such material have been removed or excluded. They are not suitable for this staging release because they exceed the data-free, public-safe boundary.

## Reconsideration

A direction may be reconsidered only through an owner decision that defines its scope, release eligibility, and safeguards. Until then, this repository should retain only general methods documentation and data-free engineering support.
